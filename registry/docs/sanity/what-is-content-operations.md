# What is content operations?

A structured foundation, automation layer, and agentic context that modern organizations need to move faster, work smarter, and power every content experience—from websites and apps to AI agents and beyond. This is content operations for the AI era.

In the real world, content operations are not linear processes. They are cyclical, with each stage informing and feeding back into the others. AI-powered automation and structured content enable intelligent workflows that adapt and improve over time. For example, analytics from content distribution might influence your governance policies, or AI-powered creation tools might change how you approach content planning.

You can think of these content operation processes in the following five stages.

## 1. Planning and governing

Model your business by building a content system that matches how your organization actually operates. This means defining adaptive content models, setting up workflows, establishing [roles and permissions](https://www.sanity.io/docs/user-guides/roles), and creating governance policies that ensure content quality and consistency. This stage is about aligning your content to your operations, not CMS limitations—building the foundation for how your organization thinks about and structures content.

## 2. Content creation and management

AI-accelerated development lets your teams focus on what matters: creating great content. Sanity provides custom content applications like [Studio](https://www.sanity.io/docs/studio) and [Canvas](https://www.sanity.io/docs/canvas) that teams actually love to use, with AI assistance built in. These structured editing environments adapt to your content model while maintaining the integrity of your data, supporting collaborative workflows, version control, and efficient content production from writing and editing to asset creation and organization.

## 3. Distributions and automation

Automate everything and power anything. Let automation handle the repetitive work while your content flows from a single source of truth to every channel where it needs to be. This includes publishing workflows, content delivery networks, and automated processes that ensure content reaches its intended audience across all touchpoints. The Content Operating System provides API-first headless delivery with [APIs, SDKs](https://www.sanity.io/docs/apis-and-sdks), and [serverless functions](https://www.sanity.io/docs/ai) that enable universal connectivity and automate distribution while maintaining content integrity. This stage is where your structured content proves its value, flowing seamlessly to websites, apps, digital displays, AI agents, and any other channel you need to reach.

## 4. Analysis and optimization

Understanding how your content performs across channels and using those insights to improve. Structured content that AI understands enables better analytics, A/B testing, and performance monitoring that help you make data-driven decisions about your content strategy. The Content Operating System provides tools to track content performance and integrate with analytics platforms, giving you visibility into how your audience engages with your content and its impact on your business goals.

## 5. Extending and integrations

Universal connectivity means your content system integrates seamlessly with every tool and platform in your tech stack. Sanity provides agentic context storage and MCP delivery, giving context to AI agents with full governance and control. Build custom integrations, extend functionality with plugins, and create specialized applications that address specific business needs.
