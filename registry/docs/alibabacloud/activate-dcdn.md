You need to activate Dynamic Content Delivery Network (DCDN) before you can use it. This topic describes how to activate DCDN.

## Prerequisites

An Alibaba Cloud account is created. To create an Alibaba Cloud account, go to the [Sign up to Alibaba Cloud](https://account.alibabacloud.com/register/intl_register.htm?spm=a2c45.11132027.495866.3.121a5455M9EN53) page.

## Procedure

1.  Log on to the [Alibaba Cloud DCDN](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/product/dcdn) page.
    
2.  Click **Buy Now**, and select a metering method.
    
    For more information about the pricing of DCDN, go to the [Pricing](https://www.alibabacloud.com/product/dcdn/pricing) page.
    
3.  Click **Buy Now**.
    
4.  On the **Confirm Order** page, select **Terms of Service**, and click **Activate Now**.
    
5.  Complete the payment to activate DCDN.
    
    After you activate DCDN, you can click **Alibaba Cloud Management Console** to go to the DCDN console.
    

## What to do next

[Add a domain name](/help/en/edge-security-acceleration/dcdn/getting-started/add-a-domain-name#task-alv-1fk-xdb)

## Deactivate or stop the billing of DCDN

If you directly deactivate DCDN, your business may be interrupted. Therefore, Alibaba Cloud does not allow you to deactivate DCDN in the console. However, if you do not add domain names to DCDN or use features such as edge security and EdgeRoutine, you are not charged for DCDN. This is equivalent to deactivating DCDN. For more information, see [How do I deactivate or stop the billing of DCDN?](/help/en/edge-security-acceleration/dcdn/support/how-do-i-deactivate-or-stop-the-billing-of-dcdn)
