Alibaba Cloud Model Studio is adjusting the validity period of the [new user free quota](/help/en/model-studio/new-free-quota) to 90 days.

## **Change description**

Starting **September 8, 3:00 UTC**, the [new user free quota](/help/en/model-studio/new-free-quota) validity period will be **90 days** for first-time Model Studio activations (Singapore region).

> **For users who activated the service before** **03:00 UTC on September 8**, the validity period remains unchanged.

> For each model's free quota validity period, see [Models](/help/en/model-studio/models).
