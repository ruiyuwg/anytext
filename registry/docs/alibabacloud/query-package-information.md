You can query your plans, such as the plan type, status, expiration time, and associated websites, to better manage the plans.

## **Procedure**

1.  Log on to the [ESA console](https://esa.console.alibabacloud.com/siteManage/list), and in the left-side navigation pane, choose **Billing Management** > **Plans**.
    
2.  Click the **Website Plans** tab to view the plan information.
