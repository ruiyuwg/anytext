Alibaba Cloud E-MapReduce (EMR) has passed multiple certifications and can meet your various compliance requirements.

**Compliance certification**

**Description**

ISO 27018

ISO 27018 establishes commonly accepted control objectives, controls, and guidelines for implementing measures to protect Personally Identifiable Information (PII). ISO 27018 specifies applicable PII requirements based on the information security risks that are described in ISO/IEC 27002.

ISO 27701

ISO 27701 is the most authoritative standard that provides guidance for establishing a Privacy Information Management System (PIMS). Alibaba Cloud is one of the first cloud service providers to pass the certification.

ISO 27799

ISO 27799 provides guidelines for healthcare organizations to protect personal health information in a professional manner.

ISO 29151

ISO 29151 provides practical guidelines for enterprises to protect personal privacy and mitigate compliance risks to meet the requirements for PII protection and security assessment.

ISO 9001

ISO 9001 is a quality management standard that helps organizations fulfill their commitment to meet the requirements of customers and regulatory requirements related to a product.

ISO 20000

ISO 20000 is the first international standard for IT service management. The cloud services that pass the ISO/IEC 20000-1:2011 certification establish and carry out standardized service processes to reduce overall IT risks.

ISO 22301

ISO 22301 is an international standard for business continuity management. ISO 22301 is the only standard that can measure whether the business continuity management capability of an enterprise can meet social responsibilities and customer commitment. Alibaba Cloud has passed the latest ISO/IEC 22301 certification.

ISO 27001

ISO 27001 is an international standard that is widely used to manage information security. Alibaba Cloud has passed the certification and fulfill its responsibilities in data security, network security, communication security, and operation security.

ISO 27017

ISO 27017 provides a set of guidelines for information security controls related to the use of cloud services, including additional implementation guidelines for controls related to ISO/IEC 27002 and additional controls and implementation guidelines related to cloud service features.

BS 10012

BS 10012 demonstrates compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). BS 10012 specifies the requirements for a personal information management system that is aligned to recognized best practices and helps organizations appropriately use personal information while respecting personal privacy and securing personal records related to individuals.

CSA STAR

CSA STAR Certification is jointly formulated by British Standards Institution (BSI) and Cloud Security Alliance (CSA). Alibaba Cloud has passed the CSA STAR Gold certification.

SOC

Service Organization Controls (SOC) reports are designed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). SOC reports provide options for service providers. This way, service providers can provide more related reports to customers.
