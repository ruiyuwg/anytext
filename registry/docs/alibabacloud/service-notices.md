## **Scheduled hardware maintenance**

On December 22, 2025, between 10:00 and 18:00 (UTC+8), Alibaba Cloud will perform scheduled hardware maintenance on the DataHub infrastructure in the China (Beijing) region. During this maintenance window, you may experience momentary service jitter. If your workloads are affected, please contact us by submitting a ticket.

Thank you for your understanding.
