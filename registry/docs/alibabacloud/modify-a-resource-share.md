A resource owner can modify the basic information of a resource share created by the resource owner and the resources, permissions, and principals added to the resource share.

## Procedure

1.  Log on to the [Resource Management console](https://resourcemanager.console.alibabacloud.com/resource-share).
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Resource Sharing** > **Resources I Share**.
3.  On the page that appears, find the desired resource share and click its ID.
4.  In the upper-right corner of the page that appears, click **Edit Resource Share**.
5.  On the **Edit Resource Share** page, modify the resource share.
    
    You can click the icon of each step on the left to quickly switch to the page where the information you want to modify resides.
    
    -   In the **Configure Basic Information and Add Resources** step, you can change the name or principal scope of the resource share, add resources to the resource share, or remove resources from the resource share.
    -   In the **Add Permissions** step, you can modify permissions added to the resource share.
        
        **Note** If only one permission is available for the types of resources in the resource share, you cannot modify the permission.
        
    -   In the **Add Principals** step, you can add principals to or remove principals from the resource share.
6.  In the **Confirm and Submit** step, click **OK**.
