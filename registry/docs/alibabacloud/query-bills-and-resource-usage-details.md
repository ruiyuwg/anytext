The price of a data transfer plan varies by its subscription duration and size.

> A data transfer plan is automatically used to offset data transfer fees that are generated by certain instances until the data transfer plan is used up or expires. Resources that are eligible for data transfer plans are Elastic Compute Service (ECS), elastic IP addresses (EIPs), Classic Load Balancer (CLB), and Internet Shared Bandwidth instances that are billed on a pay-by-data-transfer basis. Data transfer plans do not apply to EIPs of BGP (Multi-ISP) Pro. For more information, see [Supported services and data transfer types](/help/en/dtp/product-overview/what-is-a-data-transfer-plan#0bf6f99d98xuf).

> The subscription of a data transfer plan cannot be canceled.

## Specifications

Data transfer plans are available in monthly and annual subscriptions. After a data transfer plan expires, the remaining quota is cleared and cannot be transferred to other data transfer plans or refunded.

Specifications of monthly and annual subscription plans are listed in the following table.

**Duration**

**Specifications**

1 month

50 GB, 100 GB, 300 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB, 2 TB, 3 TB, 4 TB, 5 TB, 6 TB, and 7 TB

1 year

1 TB, 5 TB, and 50 TB

## **View bills**

View the bills and resource usage details of data transfer plans in the Expenses and Costs console.

### **Overview of monthly bills**

Log on to the [Expenses and Costs console](https://billing-cost.console.alibabacloud.com/fortune/billing-account). In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Billing** > **Bills** to view your monthly bills over the entire billing cycle and export the bills.

### **Billing details**

Log on to the [Expenses and Costs console](https://billing-cost.console.alibabacloud.com/fortune/billing-account). In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Billing** > **Bill Details** to view billing details by billing cycle, day, billable item or instance.

### **Usage details**

Log on to the [Expenses and Costs console](https://billing-cost.console.alibabacloud.com/fortune/billing-account). In the left-side navigation pane, click **Resource Packages**.

-   View remaining capacity: Click the **Instances** tab. Click the ![筛选](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/4348496661/p75480.png) icon next to **Product Name** and select **Data Transfer Plan**. Find the target data transfer plan and view the usage details in the **Total Capacity** and **Remaining Capacity** columns.
    
-   View usage details: Click the **Details** tab, and specify the parameters for filtering.
    
    Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6685546671/p1036728.png) button in the upper-right corner of the summary table to export. In the exported file, the usage field is recorded in bytes.
