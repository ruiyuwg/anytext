Data Transfer Plans are easy-to-use and cost-effective resource plans. After purchase, a data transfer plan takes effect immediately and automatically offsets the data transfer fees for multiple pay-by-traffic cloud services until the plan is depleted or expires.

## Before you begin

Before purchasing a data transfer plan, note the following:

-   Each Alibaba Cloud account is limited to 200 active data transfer plans. This quota cannot be increased. Select a plan size that meets your requirements.
    
-   The regions that a data transfer plan covers are as follows:
    
    Click to view the mapping between areas and regions
    
    **Data Transfer Plan Area**
    
    **Available In**
    
    Chinese Mainland
    
    China (Qingdao), China (Beijing), China (Zhangjiakou), China (Hohhot), China (Ulanqab), China (Hangzhou), China (Shanghai), China (Nanjing - Local Region), China (Wuhan - Local Region), China (Shenzhen), China (Heyuan), China (Guangzhou), China (Chengdu)
    
    **Excluded**: China (Fuzhou - Local Region)
    
    China (Hong Kong)
    
    China (Hong Kong)
    
    Outside Chinese Mainland
    
    Singapore, Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur), Indonesia (Jakarta), Philippines (Manila), Thailand (Bangkok), Japan (Tokyo), South Korea (Seoul), US (Silicon Valley), US (Virginia), Germany (Frankfurt), UK (London)
    
-   A data transfer plan offsets data transfer fees for multiple pay-by-traffic cloud products.
    
    Click to view supported products and traffic types
    
    **Product**
    
    **Traffic type**
    
    **Description**
    
    ECS
    
    IPv4

    IPv6 gateway

## Purchase a plan

1.  Log on to the [Data Transfer Plan buy page](https://common-buy-intl.aliyun.com/?spm=a3c0i.63574.1167971.3.51cb4705k4PHIy&commodityCode=flowbag_intl&accounttraceid=87c33f86-1d75-451d-a5f8-0b67c0e0135e#/buy).
    
2.  On the buy page, configure the as follows. Then, click **Buy Now** and complete the payment.
    
    **Parameter**
    
    **Description**
    
    **Traffic Package Size**
    
    Select a plan size.
    
    Available sizes vary based on the selected validity period. For specific options, see the [buy page](https://common-buy-intl.alibabacloud.com/flowpack/prepay?spm=5176.11182184.content.1.185f4882572bTZ#/buy).
    
    **Plan**
    
    Select the package for the Data Transfer Plan.
    
    -   **China mainland**: Covers regions in the China mainland.
        
    -   **Hong Kong**: Covers the China (Hong Kong) region.
        
    -   **Overseas**: Covers overseas regions.
        
    
    **Expiry Date**
    
    Select the validity period for the plan
    
    -   1 Month
        
    -   1 Year
        
    
    After a data transfer plan expires, it is not eligible for a refund. Any unused data allowance will be cleared and cannot be transferred to another plan.
    
    **Quantity**
    
    Enter the number of plans you want to purchase.
