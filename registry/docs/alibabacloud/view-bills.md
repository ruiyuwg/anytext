If you have questions about the billing of Alibaba Cloud E-MapReduce (EMR) Serverless Spark, you can view your bills and billing details in the Expenses and Costs console.

## Procedure

1.  Log on to the [Expenses and Costs](https://usercenter2-intl.console.alibabacloud.com/) console.
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose .
    
3.  On the **Bill Details** page, view billing details or export bills.
    
    For more information, see the topics in the [Bill Management](/help/en/user-center/billing-management/) directory.
