If an Express Connect router (ECR) is used for cross-region connections, data transfer bills are generated by Cloud Data Transfer (CDT). This topic describes the billing rules of cross-region data transfers over an ECR.

## **Prerequisites**

If you want to use Express Connect circuits of China Unicom to establish connections between regions in the Chinese mainland and regions outside the Chinese mainland, make sure that you have completed the cross-border compliance verification. To apply for the certificate to use the cross-border connection service of China Unicom, submit your application on the [Apply for cross-border Express Connect circuits of China Unicom](https://ecommerce.ictsoft.cn/apply/) page.

## **Billing rules**

Cross-region data transfers are classified into the following categories:

-   Local data transfers: Data is transmitted between regions in the Chinese mainland or between regions outside the Chinese mainland.
    
-   Cross-border data transfers: Data is transmitted between regions in the Chinese mainland and regions outside the Chinese mainland.
    

If you establish a cross-region connection by using an ECR, [CDT](/help/en/cdt/product-overview/what-is-cdt) bills the inbound and outbound data transfers over the connection. In hybrid cloud networking, you are charged for data transfer from a virtual private cloud (VPC) to a virtual border router (VBR) and from the VBR to the VPC. In on-premises networking, you are charged for data transfer from VBR 1 to VBR 2 and from VBR 2 to VBR 1. For more information, see [Billing overview](/help/en/cdt/billing-1) and [Inter-region data transfer](/help/en/cdt/inter-region-data-transfers).

**Note**

-   The bills of cross-account and cross-region data transfers are generated within the Alibaba Cloud account to which the ECR belongs.
    
-   Cross-region data transfers are billed based on ECRs. The data transfer allocation is not supported.
