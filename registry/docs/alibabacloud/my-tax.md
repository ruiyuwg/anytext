**For Enterprise User**

**Step 1 - Fill in your Business Registration Number**

1.  Log on to the Alibaba Cloud Management Console and click the profile picture in the upper-right corner to go to the [Account Center](https://account-console.alibabacloud.com/?#/secure) page.
    
2.  Go to **Basic Information** from the left-side navigation pane.
    
3.  Please make sure your name is your **registered business name**.
    
4.  Please fill in your 12-digit Malaysia **Business Registration Number**.
    

![image.png](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8849899171/p817136.png)

**Step 2 - Fill in your Tax Number**

1.  Log on to the Alibaba Cloud Management Console. In the top navigation bar, click **Expenses**.
    
2.  Go to **Tax ID** from the left-side navigation pane.
    
3.  Please fill in your **Tax Identification Number (TIN)**, usually in the format of 1 or 2 letter followed by 10 or 11 digits, e.g. C25163797020.
    
4.  Please fill in your **SST Registration Number** (if applicable) and provide a copy of the SST certificate.
    

![image.png](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8849899171/p817138.png)

We can only issue an invoice to you upon receipt of **all** information stated in Step 1 and Step 2. Failure to provide any of the information above will affect your ability to receive a valid e-invoice for tax deduction purpose.

**For Individual User**

**Step 1 - Fill in your Identification Number**

1.  Log on to the Alibaba Cloud Management Console and click the profile picture in the upper-right corner to go to the [Account Center](https://account-console.alibabacloud.com/?#/secure) page.
    
2.  Go to **Basic Information** from the left-side navigation pane.
    
3.  Please make sure your name is your **full name per your identification document**.
    
4.  Please fill in your **Identification Number** (i.e. 12-digit number stated in your MyKad/MyTentera/MyPR/MyKAS)
    

![image.png](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8849899171/p817135.png)
