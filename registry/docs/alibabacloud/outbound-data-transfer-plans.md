Outbound data transfer plans are subscription resource plans that are provided by Express Connect. An outbound data transfer plan takes effect only after you complete the payment.

## Prerequisites

By default, outbound data transfer plans are not available. To use outbound data transfer plans, contact your account manager to apply for the required permissions.

## Purchase and offset rules

**Rule**

**Description**

Purchase rules

-   After you purchase an outbound data transfer plan, the plan immediately takes effect and automatically offsets the outbound data transfer fees in specific regions. The validity period of an outbound data transfer plan is one year. After the outbound data transfer plan expires, the unused quota is automatically cleared.
    
-   You can purchase an unlimited number of outbound data transfer plans. If you purchased multiple outbound data transfer plans, the plan that expires first is preferentially applied.
    
    **Note**
    
    -   During the promotion period, if you purchase an outbound data transfer plan whose size is less than 50 TB and your actual usage exceeds the size of the plan by over 50 TB, you are entitled to a free quota of 50 TB. You are charged only for the part of the excess usage that exceeds 50 TB based on the actual prices of outbound data transfer.
        
    -   If you purchase an outbound data transfer plan whose size is greater than or equal to 50 TB, you cannot use the free quota.
        
    

Sizes and supported areas of outbound data transfer plans

-   Areas that support outbound data transfer plans: Chinese mainland, Asia Pacific, Indonesia, Europe & Americas, and Middle East. The price is different in different regions. For more information, visit the buy page.
    
-   Outbound data transfer plans are available in the following sizes: 1 TB, 10 TB, and 100 TB.

## Purchase outbound data transfer plans

1.  Log on to the [Express Connect Console](https://expressconnect.console.alibabacloud.com).
    
2.  In the top navigation bar, select a region. In the left-side navigation pane, click **Physical Connection**.
    
3.  In the **Purchase Outbound Data Transfer Plan** section, click **Buy Now**.
    
4.  On the **Outbound Data Transfer Plan - International Site** page, configure the following parameters and click **Buy Now**. The following table describes the parameters.
    
    **Parameter**
    
    **Description**
    
    **Quantity**
    
    The number of outbound data transfer plans that you want to purchase.
    
    **Size**
    
    The size of the outbound data transfer plan. Valid values: **1 TB**, **10 TB**, and **100 TB**.
    
    **Expiry Date**
    
    The validity period of an outbound data transfer plan, which is **1 Year**. After you complete the payment, the outbound data transfer plan immediately takes effect and automatically offsets the outbound data transfer fees in specific regions. After the outbound data transfer plan expires, the unused quota is automatically cleared.
    
    **Available In**
    
    The region in which you want to use the outbound data transfer plan. Before you purchase an outbound data transfer plan, confirm the region to which you want to apply the plan.
    
5.  On the **Confirm Order** page, read and agree to Terms of Service and click **Pay**.
    

## View the usage details of outbound data transfer plans

Before you can view the usage details of an outbound data transfer plan, you must purchase the plan.

1.  Log on to the [Express Connect Console](https://expressconnect.console.alibabacloud.com).
    
2.  In the top navigation bar, select a region. In the left-side navigation pane, click **Physical Connection**.
    
3.  In the **Purchase Outbound Data Transfer Plan** section, click **View Details**.
    
4.  On the **Manage Reserved Instances** page, you can view the basic information and usage details of outbound data transfer plans.
    
    -   On the **Instances** tab, you can view the following information about an outbound data transfer plan: owner account, name, remaining quota, effective time, and expiration time.
        
        -   In the upper-right corner of the outbound data transfer plan list, click **Customize Column Options** to specify the columns that you want to display.
            
        -   In the upper-right corner of the outbound data transfer plan list, click **Export** to export and download the list.
            
    -   On the **Details** tab, you can view the usage details of outbound data transfer plans.
        
        -   You can filter usage details by configuring the **Start Time**, **Instance Name**, or **Deducted instance ID** parameter.
            
        -   In the upper-right corner of the outbound data transfer plan list, click **Customize Column Options** to specify the columns that you want to display.
            
        -   In the upper-right corner of the outbound data transfer plan list, click **Export** to export and download the list.
