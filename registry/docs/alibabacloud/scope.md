A main account can share its coupons with its linked accounts.

# **Scope**

The coupon sharing feature is in canary release and available only to main accounts in finance trusteeship. If you want to try this feature, contact your business manager.

# **Operation guide**

## **1\. Share coupons**

Log on [User Center](https://usercenter2-intl.console.alibabacloud.com/billing/#/account/overview), click [Coupon Sharing](https://usercenter2-intl.console.alibabacloud.com/finance/union-account/share-coupons) button.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/1210234371/p888426.png)

In the row of the coupon that you want to share, click Share Management. In the Share Management dialog box that appears, select an account with which you want to share the coupon in the Applicable Account field and click Submit.

![image.png](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6976350961/p699701.png)

## **2\. View the accounts that share a coupon**

On the Coupon Sharing page, you can view the accounts that share a specific coupon in the Applicable Account column.

![image.png](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8886993961/p716144.png)

If a coupon is shared with multiple linked accounts, you can click the number in the Applicable Account column to view all applicable accounts.

### **3\. Cancel the sharing of coupons**

In the Share Management dialog box, click the X button next to the UID of the linked account for which you want to cancel the coupon sharing and click Submit. ![image.png](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/1360943071/p750618.png)
