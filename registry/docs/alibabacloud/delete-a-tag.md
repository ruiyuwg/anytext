You can downgrade predefined tags that no longer meet your business requirements to regular tags. After the downgrade, they will not be displayed in the list of predefined tags when you add tags to resources. You can also upgrade existing regular tags to predefined tags based on your business requirements.

## Downgrade a predefined tag to a regular tag

1.  Log on to the [Resource Management console](https://resourcemanager.console.alibabacloud.com/tags).
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Tag** > **Tag**.
    
3.  In the tag list, find the tag that you want to downgrade and choose **![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2955784471/p937379.png)** > **Downgrade to Non-predefined Tag** in the **Actions** column.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2955784471/p944199.png)
    
4.  In the **Downgrade to Non-predefined Tag** dialog box, click **Downgrade**.
    
    After the downgrade is successful, the value of **Predefined Tag** for the tag becomes **No**.
    

## Upgrade a regular tag to a predefined tag

1.  Log on to the [Resource Management console](https://resourcemanager.console.alibabacloud.com/tags).
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Tag** > **Tag**.
    
3.  In the tag list, find the tag that you want to upgrade and choose **![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2955784471/p937379.png)** > **Upgrade to Predefined Tag** in the **Actions** column.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2955784471/p942300.png)
    
4.  In the **Upgrade to Predefined Tag** dialog box, click **Upgrade**.
    
    After the upgrade is successful, the value of **Predefined Tag** for the tag becomes **Yes**.
