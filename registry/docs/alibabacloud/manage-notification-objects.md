The Alert Management sub-service of Managed Service for Prometheus can send alert notifications in the following ways:

-   [Phone, SMS message, or email](/help/en/prometheus/user-guide/create-and-manage-contacts)
    
-   [DingTalk chatbot](/help/en/prometheus/user-guide/create-a-dingtalk-chatbot), [WeCom chatbot](/help/en/prometheus/user-guide/create-a-wecom-chatbot), or [Lark chatbot](/help/en/prometheus/user-guide/create-a-lark-chatbot)
    
-   [Webhook, to customize notification objects](/help/en/prometheus/user-guide/create-a-custom-webhook-based-alert-contact)
    

Additionally, you can manage contacts via [contact groups](/help/en/prometheus/user-guide/contact-groups).
