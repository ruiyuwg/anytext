Identity verification confirms your legal identity with Alibaba Cloud. You must complete verification before purchasing specific cloud services deployed in the Chinese mainland, signing online contracts, or meeting other regulatory requirements.

## Why verification is required

Verification protects your account from unauthorized access and identity theft. It is also required for the following actions:

-   **Purchasing region-locked services**: Specific cloud services deployed on nodes in the Chinese mainland, and Global Accelerator (GA) services that include the Chinese mainland, require verification to comply with local laws and regulations.
    
-   **Signing online contracts**: Alibaba Cloud online contract workflows require a verified account.
    
-   **Applying for a credit limit**: Credit limit applications are available only to verified accounts.
    
-   **Joining the Alibaba Cloud Partner Network (ACPN)**: Partner enrollment requires verification.
    
-   **Changing your mobile number across countries**: Updating the mobile number on your account to one from a different country or region requires verification.
    

## Individual and enterprise verification

Verify your account as either an [individual](/help/en/account/verify-your-identity-individual-account) or an [enterprise](/help/en/account/verify-your-identity-enterprise-account/). This choice is permanent.

**Important**

Individual and enterprise verification are mutually exclusive. After verification, the account type is locked. Choose the type that matches your use case before proceeding.

## **Limitations**

**Item**

**Details**

One identity per account

Each Alibaba Cloud account can be verified for only a single entity, either an individual or an enterprise.

Permanent verification

Once complete, verification cannot be changed or removed. If the account is no longer needed, [close the account](/help/en/account/close-an-alibaba-cloud-account).

Document consistency

The issuing country or region of the verification document must match your account's Country/Region setting.

Account type lock-in

After verification, the account type (Individual or Enterprise) is locked and cannot be changed.

## **FAQ**

### Does account identity verification need to match domain name verification?

No. These are separate processes:

-   **Account identity verification** confirms your legal ownership of the Alibaba Cloud account.
    
-   **Domain name verification** confirms ownership of a specific domain. It is required when the domain registrant is an organization (such as a company, foundation, or school) and the region is the Chinese mainland. A domain name cannot be parsed without this verification. For details, see [How to complete identity verification for a domain name](/help/en/dws/user-guide/how-to-complete-domain-name-authentication).
    

One account can manage multiple domains, each verified under different entities.
