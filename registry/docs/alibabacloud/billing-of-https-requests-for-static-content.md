After you enable HTTPS in Alibaba Cloud CDN, you are charged for HTTPS requests for static content based on the pay-as-you-go billing method.

## **Query the usage**

### **Pay-as-you-go**

1.  Log on to the [Alibaba Cloud CDN](https://cdn.console.alibabacloud.com) console.
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, click **Usage**.
    
3.  On the **Usage** page, select **NUMBER OF HTTPS REQUESTS** and click **Search**.
