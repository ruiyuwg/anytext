The data catalog provides a unified interface for managing AI Catalog metadata. This topic describes how to manage dataset and model metadata in the data catalog.

## **Go to the AI Catalog data catalog page**

1.  Go to the [Workspaces](https://dataworks.console.aliyun.com/workspace/list) page in the DataWorks console. In the top navigation bar, select a desired region. Find the desired workspace and choose **Shortcuts** > **Data Studio** in the **Actions** column.
    
2.  In the navigation pane on the left, click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/7618107371/p840002.png) icon. Then, under **Data Catalog**, click **AI Catalog** to open the AI Catalog management page.
    

## **Manage the AI Catalog data catalog**

You can manage dataset and model objects in the AI Catalog data catalog.

### **View public datasets**

1.  Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8025062571/p981275.png) icon next to AI Catalog. In the data catalog, navigate to **Dataset** > **Public Dataset**.
    
2.  Click **Public Dataset** to open the **Public Dataset** page.
    
3.  In the search box, enter a keyword for the dataset name to find the target dataset.
    
4.  After the search is complete, click a dataset name to open its details page and view the **Basic Information**, such as **Size** and **Data Volume**.
    
5.  To view detailed information about the dataset, click **View** next to **Dataset Introduction** on the **Basic Information** tab of the dataset details page.
    

### **Manage custom datasets**

**Create a custom dataset**

1.  Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8025062571/p981275.png) icon next to AI Catalog. In the data catalog, navigate to **Dataset** > **Custom Dataset**.
    
2.  Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8025062571/p980334.png) icon next to Custom Dataset to open the [PAI console](https://pai.console.alibabacloud.com/).
    
3.  On the **Custom** tab of **AI Asset Management** > **Dataset**, you can [create a custom dataset](/help/en/pai/user-guide/create-and-manage-datasets).
    

**Manage a custom dataset**

1.  View a custom dataset.
    
    1.  Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8025062571/p981275.png) icon next to AI Catalog. In the data catalog, navigate to **Dataset** > **Custom Dataset**.
        
    2.  Click **Custom Dataset** to open the **Custom Dataset** list page.
        
    3.  In the search box, enter a keyword for the dataset name to find the target dataset.
        
    4.  When the search is complete, click a dataset name to open its details page. Here, you can view the **Basic Information**, such as **Type** and **Visibility**.
        
    5.  On the **Basic Information** tab, click the **Dataset ID** to view more details.
        
2.  To update or delete a custom dataset, see [Manage datasets in PAI](/help/en/pai/user-guide/create-and-manage-datasets#7e62c02c183bk).
    

### **Manage models**

**Register a model**

1.  Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8025062571/p981275.png) icon next to AI Catalog. In the data catalog, find Model.
    
2.  To the right of **Model**, click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8025062571/p980334.png) icon to open the [PAI console](https://pai.console.alibabacloud.com/console).
    
3.  On the **AI Asset Management** > **Model** page, [register a model](/help/en/pai/user-guide/register-and-manage-models#title-abh-neg-eim).
    

**Manage a model**

1.  View a model.
    
    1.  Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8025062571/p981275.png) icon next to AI Catalog. In the data catalog, find Model.
        
    2.  Click **Model** to open the **Model** list page.
        
    3.  In the search box, enter a keyword for the model name to find the target model.
        
    4.  After the search is complete, click a **Model Name** to open its details page. You can view the **Basic Information**, such as **Latest Version** and **Visibility**.
        
    5.  To view more details, click the **Model ID** on the **Basic Information** tab of the model details page.
        
2.  To delete a model, see [Manage models in PAI](/help/en/pai/user-guide/register-and-manage-models#title-2uw-jjg-ppm).
