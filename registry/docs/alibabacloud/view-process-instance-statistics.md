Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) can collect workflow statistics. This provides an overview of approval workflows and allows you to view approval details.

## **Prerequisites**

An approval workflow is configured. For more information, see [Configure an approval workflow](/help/en/sase/user-guide/manage-the-approval-process).

## **Procedure**

1.  Log on to the [SASE console](https://yundun.console.alibabacloud.com/?p=csas).
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Approval Center** > **Workflow Instance**.
    
3.  In the Workflow Statistics section, view the workflow trend chart, the workflow distribution by type, and the number of organizational workflows within the last 1 day, last 7 days, and last month.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/1933620371/p865622.png)
    

## **View the status of workflows**

SASE displays the workflows that are in process and are complete. You can search for specific workflows and view workflow details based on your business requirements.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/9352059271/p810664.png)

## **Process a workflow**

On the **Processing** tab, click **Batch Approve** or **Batch Reject** to approve or reject a workflow.
