If you forget your Alibaba Cloud account password, follow these steps to reset it online.

## **Prerequisites**

You will need your **account name** or **email** to verify your identity. If the email address or secure phone number is unavailable, update your email by submitting an appeal. For more information, see [Account mailbox is unavailable](/help/en/account/account-email-unavailable).

You cannot reset your password by signing in with Google or GitHub.

## **Reset your password**

1.  Go to the [Alibaba Cloud homepage](https://www.alibabacloud.com/zh?_p_lc=1&spm=5176.2020520207.top-nav.dlogo.4b244c12lGAT05), click **Log On** in the upper-right corner, and select **Forgot Password**.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6919607271/p836653.png)
    
2.  On the **Retrieve Password** page, enter your **account name** or **email address as** the Login ID, and the verification code. Click **Submit**.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6919607271/p836655.png)
    
3.  Choose a method to verify your identity. Depending on your account's security settings, you can choose to receive a verification code by either SMS or email.
    
    -   If the email address or secure phone for your account is unavailable, first see [How to update your account email address](/help/en/account/account-email-unavailable) to submit an appeal and update it.
        
    -   If you're having trouble receiving the verification code, see [Why am I unable to receive a verification code?](/help/en/account/no-verification-code-received-during-registration-or-logon-verification) for troubleshooting information.![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6919607271/p836713.png)
        
4.  Enter the verification code you receive. On the Reset Password page, create and confirm your new password. The password must meet the following requirements:
    
    -   Length: 8 to 20 characters
        
    -   Can contain uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Spaces are not allowed. Must include at least three of the following character types: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6919607271/p836715.png)
        

1.  After the password is reset, use it to log on to your Alibaba Cloud account. To improve account security, store your new password in a safe place and change it periodically.![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6919607271/p836716.png)
    

## **What to do next**

Keep your new password secure and update it regularly. For more information, see [Change the password for an Alibaba Cloud account](/help/en/account/how-do-i-change-my-account-password).
