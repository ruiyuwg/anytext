Message notification policies vary depending on the type of SSL certificate. You can use all message notification features for free with commercial certificates. However, to customize notifications for uploaded certificates, you must purchase message notification resources. This topic describes the billing details for the message notification service and how to purchase message notification resources.

## **Procedure**

1.  Log on to the [Certificate Management Service console](https://yundun.console.alibabacloud.com/?p=cas).
    
2.  In the navigation pane on the left, choose **Certificate and Domain Application Services** > ****Notification****.
    
3.  On the **Notification** page, click **Purchase** below **Unused Notification Quota**.
    
4.  On the **Certificate Value-added Service** purchase page, select the message resources that you want to purchase, click **Buy Now**, and complete the payment.
    
    **Parameter**
    
    **Description**
    
    **Service type**
    
    By default, **Customized message reminder** is selected.
    
    **Method of purchase**
    
    Only Purchase by package is supported.
    
    **Package**
    
    The **quota** for notifications. The unit price of a notification policy varies based on the specification of the plan.
    
    **Quantity**
    
    The number of plans that you want to purchase. You can purchase up to 100 plans.
    
    **Time**
    
    The default value is One year.
    
    The subscription duration of the notification feature is determined by the validity period of the associated certificate. The maximum subscription duration is one year.
    

## **Expiration**

The validity period of the notification feature is determined by the validity period of the associated certificate. After the associated certificate expires, the feature is automatically disabled.
