This topic describes how to view the information about a folder, such as the name, ID, creation time, RDPath, and members of the folder.

## Procedure

1.  Log on to the [Resource Management console](https://resourcemanager.console.alibabacloud.com/) as a management account.
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Resource Directory** > **Management**.
    
3.  In the upper-right corner of the page that appears, click **Resource Organization View**.
    
4.  In the left-side navigation tree, click the folder whose information you want to view. In the right-side pane that appears, click **View Details and Perform More Operations** in the upper-right corner.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2778753471/p936820.png)
    
    -   In the **Basic Information** section, view the name, ID, creation time, and RDPath of the folder.
        
        **Note**
        
        For more information about an RDPath, see [Terms](/help/en/resource-management/resource-directory/product-overview/resource-directory-overview#section-o5w-14l-nah).
        
    -   On the **Member** tab, view the members in the folder.
        
    -   On the **Policy** tab, view the access control policies attached to the folder.
        
    -   On the **Contact** tab, view the contacts bound to the folder.
