PolarDB offers billing methods for compute nodes: pay-as-you-go, and subscription. Each method has a different pricing plan.

## Introduction and comparison

The following table compares the billing methods.

**Billing method**

**Fee calculation for compute nodes**

**Benefits**

**Scenarios**

Pay-as-you-go

Fees are generated based on the actual computing power used and are deducted from your account on an hourly basis.

Flexible

Small workloads with infrequent and minor fluctuations.

Subscription

Pay for several months of computing power in advance when you create a cluster.

Low-cost

Large workloads with infrequent and minor fluctuations.
