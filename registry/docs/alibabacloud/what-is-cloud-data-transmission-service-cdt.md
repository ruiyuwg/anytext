Cloud Data Transfer (CDT) is an Alibaba Cloud service that manages the billing of bandwidth resources. CDT supports flexible billing and metering methods, such as free quotas, pay-by-data-transfer, and tiered pricing models, to help you reduce bandwidth costs.

-   CDT supports tiered pricing for Internet data transfers per month in each region. The unit price decreases as the amount of Internet data transfers increases.
    
-   CDT supports the pay-by-data-transfer metering method for inter-region data transfers. This metering method is more flexible, and allows you to enable inter-region communication based on business requirements.
    

## **Benefits**

-   **Free data transfers**: Beginning June 1, 2025, the monthly quota on free Internet data transfers provided by CDT is increased from 200 GB to 220 GB. Among the 220 GB of free Internet data transfers, 20 GB can be used in **regions in the Chinese mainland**, and the other 200 GB can be used only in **regions outside the Chinese mainland**.
    
-   **Cost effective**: CDT supports [tired pricing](/help/en/cdt/internet-data-transfers/#370813ffd2hov) for Interment data transfers. The unit price decreases as the amount of Internet data transfer increases. The tiered pricing model determines the pricing tier based on the total amount of data transfers accumulated from multiple services and bills data transfers for each region.
    
-   **Flexible metering method**: CDT supports the pay-by-data-transfer metering method for inter-region data transfers. This prevents resource waste because you only need to pay for the resources that you use.
    
-   **Multiple supported services**: For more information, see [Supported services](/help/en/cdt/product-overview/supported-services-and-billing-methods#DAS).
    
-   **Support for Financial Operations (FinOps):** CDT allows you to use FinOps to separate resource creation from resource billing. This improves delivery efficiency. CDT can bill Internet data transfers and inter-region data transfers for different Alibaba Cloud services. CDT also allows you to add tags to resources and split the bills of data transfers that are generated by the resources based on the tags. This improves the collaboration efficiency of the business, ITT, and finance departments, and helps you gain more financial control and predictability.
    

## Enable CDT

Log on to the [CDT console](https://cdt.console.alibabacloud.com/cdt/ap-southeast-5/list), find the service for which you want to enable CDT, click **Upgrade**, and then complete the configurations. For more information, see [Getting started](/help/en/cdt/user-guide/upgrade-to-cdt-billing).

**Note**

-   Beginning 00:00:00 on December 12, 2024, you can use CDT without the need to activate CDT.
    
-   After you enable CDT for cloud services, all existing and new pay-by-data-transfer instances are billed by CDT. Pay-by-bandwidth instances continue to be billed by the original Alibaba Cloud services. You can query your CDT bills on the Bill Details page in the Expenses and Costs console.
    
-   After you activate CDT, CDT allocates you a quota on free Internet data transfers of 220 GB per month. Among the 220 GB of free Internet data transfers, 20 GB can be used in **regions in the Chinese mainland**, and the other 200 GB can be used only in **regions outside the Chinese mainland**.
