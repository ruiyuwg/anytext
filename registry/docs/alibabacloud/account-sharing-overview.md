This topic describes the features of account sharing, its use cases, and how to enable it.

## **What is account sharing?**

Account sharing allows an enterprise administrator to manage and share assets across accounts within an enterprise. You must enable the [Enterprise Account Center](https://ea.console.aliyun.com/account-manage/) to use this feature.

After you enable the Enterprise Account Center, log on to the console and go to **Expenses and Costs** > **Billing Account** > **[Account Sharing](https://billing-cost-intl.aliyun.com/fortune/union-account/ri-allocation)** to use the feature.

## Enable account sharing

Account sharing is automatically enabled once you set up the Enterprise Account Center. Follow these steps to get started:

1.  Complete enterprise real-name verification. .
    
2.  Enable the Enterprise Account Center. .
    
3.  Invite member accounts to your resource directory..
    
4.  Structure accounts into organizational units.
    
5.  Manage subaccounts via the resource directory.
    

## **Main features**

Account sharing provides the following main features:

### **Unified account management**

This feature lets you manage authorized accounts and their assets. You can perform the following actions:

-   **Available balance**: View the available balance of authorized accounts.
    
-   **Credit limit**: View and modify the credit limit of authorized accounts.
    
-   **Low balance alert**: View and modify low balance alerts for authorized accounts.
    
-   **Fund transfers**: Transfer and reclaim cash balances between accounts and view transfer records.
    
-   **Account details**: Access the details page of authorized accounts.
    

For more information, see [Unified account management](/help/en/user-center/unified-account-management).

### **Equity asset sharing**

Equity assets include savings plans, coupons, and reserved capacity that represent prepaid value or discounts. After enabling the Enterprise Account Center, you can share your enterprise's equity assets with subaccounts.

-   **Supported sharable resources**: The management account must share these assets with subaccounts via resource sharing. Subaccounts then gain access automatically.
    
    -   **Savings plans**: For a list of supported cloud services, see [Cloud products that support savings plans](/help/en/user-center/what-is-the-saving-plan#4af32e903084m).
        
    -   **Reserved instance vouchers**: For limitations, see [Notes about reserved instances](/help/en/user-center/resource-package-instance-management#836bc8b3c2955).
        
    -   **Storage Capacity Units (SCUs)**: For limitations, see [Notes about SCUs](/help/en/user-center/resource-package-instance-management#836bc8b3c2955).
        
    -   **Coupons**: For usage scope, see [Use and manage coupons](/help/en/user-center/how-to-use-coupons#711087cc87dkx).
        
-   **Supported sharing scopes**:
    
    -   **Share with accounts**: The sharing scope is fixed to specific accounts and only changes if you manually adjust it.
        
    -   **Share with an organization**: The sharing scope dynamically adjusts as accounts are added to or removed from the organizational unit.
        
-   **Supported sharing methods**:
    
    -   **Share by specific asset ID**: Set a sharing scope for an individual asset, which can be shared with specific accounts or organizational units.
        
    -   **Set automatic sharing rules by asset type**: New assets of a specific type are automatically shared according to your pre-configured rules.
        

For more information, see [Equity asset sharing](/help/en/user-center/equity-asset-sharing).
