This topic describes how to view the basic information about a resource directory, including its ID, creation time, management account, and enterprise name.

## **Procedure**

1.  Log on to the [Resource Management console](https://resourcemanager.console.alibabacloud.com/) with a management account.
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Resource Directory** > **Settings**.
    
3.  View the basic information about the resource directory, including its ID, creation time, management account, and enterprise name.
