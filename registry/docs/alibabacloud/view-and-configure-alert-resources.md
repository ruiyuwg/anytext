The Alert Resource page lets you view the alert resources for your account in a specific Region and configure daily upper limits for Alert SMS and phone calls.

## Billing

DataWorks provides a monthly Free Quota for Alert SMS and phone calls based on your DataWorks edition. Alerts within this quota are free. Usage beyond the Free Quota is charged on a pay-as-you-go basis. For more information, see [Billing for Alert SMS and phone calls](/help/en/dataworks/resource-costs#996d5441841t2).

## View alert resources

1.  [Go to the DataWorks console](https://dataworks.console.aliyun.com/overview).
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, click **Others** > **Alert Resource**.
    
3.  In the upper-left corner, select the Region where your Workspace is located to view that Region's alert resource usage.
    
    On the **Alert Resource** page, you can view the **Daily Usage of Alert Resources** and **Alert Resource Usage in Current Month** sections:
    
    -   By default, the **Daily Usage of Alert Resources** section displays the number of Alert SMS and phone calls generated today by modules such as **Data Integration**, **Intelligent Monitoring**, **Data Quality**, and **Code Review**. You can switch dates to view resource usage for a specific day.
        
    -   The **Alert Resource Usage in Current Month** section displays monthly statistics, including **Total Usage in This Month**, **Free Quota**, **Used Free Quota**, **Remaining Free Quota**, and **Pay-as-you-go Resource Usage**.
        
        The **Free Quota** displayed here depends on your DataWorks edition. For more information, see [Billing for Alert SMS and phone calls](/help/en/dataworks/resource-costs#996d5441841t2).
        
    

## Set daily alert limits

1.  In the **Daily Usage of Alert Resources** section, click **Specify Upper Limits for Alerts** in the upper-right corner.
    
2.  In the **Specify Upper Limits for Alerts** dialog box, configure **Daily Maximum Text Messages for Alerting** and **Daily Maximum Phone Calls for Alerting**.
    
    You can configure upper limits for alerts to control costs. By default, the daily upper limits for Alert SMS and phone calls are set to **Unlimited**. You can adjust these limits as needed.
    
    **Important**
    
    -   The upper limits you configure apply only to the current Region.
        
    -   After the upper limits are reached, you will no longer receive Alert SMS or phone calls.
        
    
3.  Click **Confirm**.
