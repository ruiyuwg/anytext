Valued Alibaba Cloud customers, Alibaba Cloud made some adjustments to the billable items of Classic Load Balancer (CLB) to increase the billing transparency and accuracy.

## **Effective time**

00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024.

## **Adjustment**

The following adjustments are made to the billable items of CLB:

-   **Change of billable item name**: **instance fee** is renamed **public IP retention fee**. The unit price of the billable item remains unchanged. You are charged public IP retention fees only for Internet-facing CLB instances. This adjustment aims to increase the billing accuracy and help you keep track of your expenses.
    
-   **New billable item: instance fee**. The unit price is **USD 0.021 per hour**.
    
    **Note**
    
    -   The instance fee applies to all Internet-facing and internal-facing CLB instances that are created after 00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024.
        
    -   CLB instances that are created before 00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024 are billed based on the previous billable items until 23:59:59 (UTC+8), November 30, 2025.

## **FAQ**

1.  **Why are the adjustments made?**
    
    -   To help you keep track of your expenses and adapt to market demands.
        
    -   To improve the stability, security, and efficiency of CLB.
        
2.  Is the billing of CLB instances created before **00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024** **changed after the adjustments?**
    
    A new billable item called instance fee is added to the bills of CLB instances created before 00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024. However, the fee of the billable item is USD 0. The actual amount is not affected before 23:59:59 (UTC+8), November 30, 2025.
    
3.  **Am I charged instance fees if I change the configurations of CLB instances that are created before** **00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024****?**
    
    Instance fees are charged only for CLB instances that are created after 00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024. The billing of CLB instances created before 00:00:00 (UTC+8), December 1, 2024 is not affected until 23:59:59 (UTC+8), November 30, 2025.
