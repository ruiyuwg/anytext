This topic describes billing for PolarDB for cloud-native databases. Learn about billing methods, billable items, overdue payments, and renewals.

PolarDB for cloud-native databases uses a storage-compute decoupled architecture. When you use a PolarDB cluster, compute nodes and storage space are billed separately.

-   **Billing methods**
    
    PolarDB for cloud-native databases provides three billing methods to meet your needs in different scenarios: subscription, pay-as-you-go, and Serverless. For more information, see [Billing methods](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/billing-type).
    
    -   Subscription: An upfront payment model. When you create a cluster, you select resources with defined specifications and pay for them in advance. The longer the subscription period, the larger the discount. This method is suitable for businesses with stable, long-term needs.
        
    -   Pay-as-you-go: A pay-as-you-go model. When you create a cluster, you select resources with defined specifications. You do not need to pay in advance. You are billed for the resources that you use. This method is suitable for businesses with flexible needs.
        
    -   [Serverless](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/user-guide/serverless-overview): A pay-as-you-go model. When you create a cluster, you do not need to select resources with defined specifications or pay in advance. PolarDB clusters can dynamically scale resources based on workload changes. This method is suitable for businesses with fluctuating, uncertain, or unpredictable workloads.
        
    
    If the billing method for your PolarDB cluster no longer meets your business requirements, you can [switch the billing method](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/conversion-payment-type) at any time.
    
-   **Billable items**
    
    Billable items for PolarDB for cloud-native databases include [compute nodes](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/compute-nodes), [storage space](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/storage-space), [backup storage (for usage that exceeds the free quota)](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/backup-storage-beyond-free-quota), [SQL Explorer (optional)](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/sql-insight-optional), and [cold data archiving (optional)](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/cold-data-archiving-optional). For more information, see [Billable items](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/billing-item/).
    
-   **Bills**
    
    You can go to **[Expenses and Costs](https://usercenter2-intl.console.alibabacloud.com/billing/#/account/overview)** > **Billing Management** > **Bill Details** to view bills and usage details for PolarDB and better understand your expenses. For more information, see [Bills](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/expense-bill).
    
-   **Expiration or overdue payment**
    
    If your PolarDB cluster expires or your Alibaba Cloud account has an overdue payment, the normal operation of the PolarDB cluster is affected. Renew the cluster or add funds to your account promptly. For more information, see [Overdue payments](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/description-of-due-or-arrears).
    
-   **Renewal**
    
    PolarDB for cloud-native databases supports auto-renewal and manual renewal. For more information, see [Renewal policy](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/renewal-description).
    
    **Note**
    
    After a subscription cluster expires, its normal operation is affected. If you want to continue to use the PolarDB cluster, renew it promptly.
