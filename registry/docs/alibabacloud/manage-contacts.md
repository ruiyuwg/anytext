The alert management feature of Application Real-Time Monitoring Service (ARMS) sends alert notifications to contacts by phone, text message, email, DingTalk, WeCom, Lark, or Webhook.

You can add the contacts and chatbots to a contact group. After you create a [notification policy](/help/en/arms/alarm-operation-center/create-and-manage-notification-policies#concept-rr3-55h-hhb) and configure the contact group as a notification object, notifications are sent to all the contacts in the group when an alert is triggered. For more information, see [Contact groups](/help/en/arms/alarm-operation-center/contact-groups#concept-84436-zh).
