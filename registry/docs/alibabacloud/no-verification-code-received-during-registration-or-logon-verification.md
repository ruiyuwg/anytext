If you cannot receive a verification code by phone or email during account registration or logon, you will be unable to complete the process. This guide provides steps to resolve this issue.

## **Overview**

To receive verification codes, first check your phone and email for device-related problems. Try to **resend** the code. If the problem persists, submit a manual appeal and our customer service team will assist you.

## **Step 1: Troubleshoot your device**

**For SMS verification:**

-   Check for blockers: Ensure that no security applications on your phone are blocking SMS messages.
    
-   Contact your carrier: Your mobile carrier might be blacklisting verification messages. Contact them to check for and remove any blocks.
    
-   Check your service status: If your service was suspended for non-payment, it may take up to 24 hours to be fully restored after payment.
    
-   Check your signal: Make sure your phone has a stable cellular signal. Network delays can sometimes interfere with delivery. Wait a few moments and try again.
    
-   Your phone may not be able to receive text messages longer than 160 English characters.
    

**For email verification:**

-   Check your spam/junk folder: Verification emails from Alibaba Cloud are sometimes mistakenly filtered as spam.
    
-   Check your email provider's blocklist: Contact your email service provider to confirm that they are not blocking emails from the **alibabacloud.com** domain.
    

If the troubleshooting steps above do not work, please submit an appeal and our support team will gladly assist you.

## Step 2: Submit an appeal

If you cannot receive verification codes on your email or secure phone during registration or logon, the appeal process consists of two steps: submitting your information and confirming the result.

### **1\.** Submit appeal information

1.  Go to the [Alibaba Cloud home page](https://www.alibabacloud.com/zh?_p_lc=1&spm=a2c45.11132017.9219519220.1.7a51525cqMDPnC), click **Log In** in the upper-right corner, and then click **Other Sign In Difficulties?**.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/7129607271/p836927.png)
    
2.  Select your scenario: **I am unable to receive verification code when registering** or **I cannot log in (MFA/TOTP and login mask could not be verified)**. Then, click **Next step**.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/9403318571/p1007478.png)
    
3.  Complete the slider verification, fill in the Appeal Materials, and click **Submit**. Note: Please provide an active email address and a valid phone number. The result of your appeal will be sent to this email.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/7129607271/p837190.png)
    

### **2\.** Confirm the appeal result

1.  After submission, you will receive a query code. An Alibaba Cloud support engineer will contact you to verify your identity and help resolve the issue.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/7129607271/p836978.png)
    

## **What to do next**

1.  Change your secure phone number: If you frequently have issues receiving SMS codes, it may be due to your local carrier. Consider [changing the phone number associated with your account](/help/en/account/specify-or-change-a-bound-mobile-phone-number).
    
2.  Configure [Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)](/help/en/account/configure-account-mfa): Binding a virtual MFA device (like Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator) provides a reliable alternative to SMS codes for identity verification in most scenarios.
