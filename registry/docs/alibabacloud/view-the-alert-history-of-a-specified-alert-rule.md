You can view the alert history and resources that triggered alerts of a specific alert rule. This way, you can identify and handle exceptions at the earliest opportunity.

## Procedure

1.  Log on to the [CloudMonitor console](https://cloudmonitor.console.alibabacloud.com/).
    
2.  In the left-side navigation pane, choose **Alerts** > **Alert Rules**.
    
3.  Find the alert rule that you want to view and click **Alert History** in the **Actions** column.
    
    You can view the details, alert contact group, alert history, and resource of the alert rule.
