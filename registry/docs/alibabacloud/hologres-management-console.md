Hologres is a real-time interactive analytics service developed by Alibaba Cloud. Hologres is compatible with PostgreSQL and seamlessly integrates with the big data ecosystem. You can use Hologres to analyze petabytes of data with high concurrency and low latency. It allows you to use business intelligence (BI) tools to analyze data and explore business. This topic describes the features provided on the Overview page of the Hologres console.

To meet your refined requirements for managing Hologres instances, Alibaba Cloud provides the [Hologres console](https://hologram.console.alibabacloud.com/#/instance) that is independent of the DataWorks console. You can use your Alibaba Cloud account to log on to the Hologres console. Then, click **Overview** in the left-side navigation pane. On the Overview page, you can create instances and view product news.

The **Overview** page of the Hologres console provides the following features:

-   **User Guide**
    
    In the User Guide section of the **Overview** page, you can click **Buy Now** to go to the buy page of Hologres. Hologres supports two billing methods: **pay-as-you-go** and **subscription**. For more information, see [Create an instance](/help/en/hologres/getting-started/purchase-a-hologres-instance#task-1918224).
    
-   **Latest Updates**
    
    In the Latest Updates section of the Overview page, you can click the Quick Start, Best Practices, Annual Report, Technology Sharing, and User Case tabs to view product news of Hologres.
    
-   **Shortcuts**
    
    In the **Shortcuts** section of the **Overview** page, you can click the buttons to go to the corresponding pages.
    
-   **Important Announcements**
    
    In the **Important Announcements** section of the **Overview** page, you can view the announcements about Hologres.
    
-   **Documentation**
    
    In the **Documentation** section of the **Overview** page, you can click the links to obtain guidance from the related product documentation pages.
