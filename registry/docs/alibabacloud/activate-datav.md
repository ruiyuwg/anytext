This topic describes how to activate DataV.

## Procedure

1.  Go to the [DataV console](https://datav.alibabacloud.com/) .
2.  Read the DataV Agreement of Service, select **I have read and agree to DataV Agreement of Service**, and click **Activate Now**.
    
    **Note**
    
    -   DataV provides a 30-day free trial. You can also purchase a new version of DataV during the use period.
        
    -   If DataV has been activated, you can perform operations in the console.
        
    
3.  On the service activation page, click **Buy**.
    
    **Note**
    
    You can also click **DataV console** in the upper-right corner to log on to the DataV Enterprise Edition console for a trial.
    
4.  On the buy page, specify **Edition** and **Time**.
    
    Product versions include **Basic Edition**, **Enterprise Edition**, and **Professional Edition**. The features of each version are different. You can click the tab of each edition to view the edition features and select an edition based on your business requirements.
    
    **Note**
    
    The **Enterprise Edition** and **Professional Edition** can be purchased on a yearly or monthly basis. The **Basic Edition** must be purchased on a yearly basis.
    
5.  Click **Buy Now**.
    

## What to do next

After you purchase a project, you can log on to the DataV console and use the [template to build a visualization application](/help/en/datav/datav-6-0/getting-started/template-overview) or [a blank canvas to build a visualization application](/help/en/datav/datav-6-0/getting-started/blank-template-overview).
