This topic describes how to configure a format for time conversion in Simple Log Service.

1.  Log on to the [Simple Log Service console](https://sls.console.alibabacloud.com).
    
2.  In the Projects section, click the project that you want to manage.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/0052190171/p768895.png)
    
3.  In the left-side navigation tree, choose **Resources > Machine Groups**. Move the pointer over the machine group that you want to manage and choose **![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6188262171/p788785.png)** > **Details**.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/1550642171/p785194.png)
    
4.  In the **Applied Logtail Configs** section, click the Logtail configuration that you want to manage.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/1550642171/p785195.png)
    
5.  On the **Logtail Configuration** page, click **Modify** in the upper-right corner. Turn off **Use System Time**, configure the time format based on your business requirements, and then click **Save** in the upper-right corner.
    
    **Note**
    
    For more information about the supported time formats, see [Time formats](/help/en/sls/time-formats).
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/1550642171/p785197.png)
