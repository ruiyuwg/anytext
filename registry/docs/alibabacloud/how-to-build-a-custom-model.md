In actual business scenarios, you may need to optimize the algorithms and adjust the structures of models. In this case, a built-in model may not meet actual business requirements. Therefore, PolarDB for AI allows you to build a custom model and run the model in confidential containers. This further ensures the data security of your model.

To build and use a custom model, do the following steps:

-   [Create a model](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/model-management#section-qn7-2b6-e72)
    
-   [Evaluate a model](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/model-management#section-ald-y92-4gh)
    
-   [Perform model inference](/help/en/polardb/polardb-for-mysql/model-management#section-tj6-52f-tgd)
