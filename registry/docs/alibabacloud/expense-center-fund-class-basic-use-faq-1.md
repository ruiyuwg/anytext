## **Basic concepts and** navigation

### What does Available Credit mean and how is it calculated?

Your **Available Credit** represents the total amount you can spend in your Alibaba Cloud account. It is a combination of your **Cash Balance**, **credit limit**, and any **credit refunds**. You can view your current Available Credit and its calculation formula on the [Billing Account](https://billing-cost.console.alibabacloud.com/fortune/billing-account) page within the **Expenses and Costs** console. The formula shows the relationship between your Available Credit, credit limit, and unsettled charges.

### What is the difference between an Account ID and a UID in Alibaba Cloud?

An **Account ID** identifies your billing account within the new **Expenses and Costs** console, whereas a **UID** identifies your Alibaba Cloud account itself.

In most cases, the Account ID is a background identifier that you can ignore. For more details, see [What is a billing account?](/help/en/user-center/fund-account-overview)

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6330908571/p1007801.png)

**Important**

When you set up a **Payment Account** in the **User Center**, the system uses the **Account ID** in the "Payment Account" field, not the UID. To avoid confusion and select the correct account, always verify it using the **Logon Name**.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/6330908571/p1007805.png)

### How do I find my income and expenditure details in the Expenses and Costs console?

You can find your income and expenditure details on the **Billing Account** page.

Follow these steps:

1.  Go to the [Billing Account](https://usercenter2-intl.console.alibabacloud.com/fortune/billing-account) page in the **Expenses and Costs** console.
    
2.  In the **Available Credit** section, find your **Cash Balance**.
    
3.  Click the **Income and Expenditure Details** link located to the right of your Cash Balance.
