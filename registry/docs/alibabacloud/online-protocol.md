Online agreements are electronic legal agreements between you and Alibaba Cloud that govern product use, platform rules, and privacy protection. You can search for these agreements by name, type, or record ID for signing.

### **View an agreement**

1.  Log on to [**Expenses and Costs**](https://billing-cost.console.alibabacloud.com/fortune/billing-account).
    
2.  In the left navigation pane, choose **Contracts** > **Online Agreement**.
    
3.  On the page that appears, enter an **Agreement Name**, select an **Agreement Type**, or enter a **Record ID for Signing**, then click **Search**. You can find various agreement types, such as product agreements, platform legal documents, and privacy policies.
    
4.  In the **Actions** column, click **Latest Version** to view the details of the agreement.
    
5.  In the **Sign Agreement** column, click **Download** to download the version of the agreement that you signed.![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/9501846371/p894110.png)
