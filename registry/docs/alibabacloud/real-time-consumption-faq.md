This topic provides answers to some frequently asked questions about real-time consumption.

## Why is the real-time consumption rate unable to reach the upper read and write limit of a shard?

-   The logic of your data transformation rules (old version) is complicated.
    
-   If data is transmitted across regions in the data transformation (old version) process, the data consumption rate varies based on the Internet condition. If the network condition is poor, the data consumption rate may be affected.
