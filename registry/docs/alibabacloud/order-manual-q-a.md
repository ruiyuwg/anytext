## **My orders**

### **Why can't I find my recent order on the My Orders page?**

Your order might be for a resource plan. On the My Orders page, switch to the **Resource Plan Orders** tab to find the order.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/0450846371/p892121.png)

### **How do I view the discount and payment details for an order?**

On the [My Orders](https://billing-cost-intl.aliyun.com/order/list) page, find the target order, and click **Details** in the Actions column to view the order details.

### What should I do if a payment times out during checkout?

On the [My Orders](https://billing-cost-intl.aliyun.com/order/list) page of the Expenses and Costs console, filter for **Unpaid** orders in the **Order Status** column and click **Pay** to complete the payment.

### My order status is 'Unpaid' even after I paid. What should I do?

If you topped up your account balance, you must use the balance to pay for the order.

### **When is an unpaid order automatically canceled?**

On the [My Orders](https://usercenter2.aliyun.com/order/list) page, find the target order and click **Details** in the **Actions** column to view the automatic cancellation time.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2854318571/p1007975.png)

### Can a canceled order be restored?

No, a canceled order cannot be restored. If you still need the product or service, you must create a new order.

### **How are orders canceled?**

Orders may be canceled for the following reasons:

-   Customers can manually cancel unpaid orders.
    
-   Automatic cancellation: The system automatically cancels an order if it is not paid before the deadline.
    
    The payment deadline varies by product. To find the deadline for a specific order, go to the [My Orders](https://usercenter2-intl.console.alibabacloud.com/order/list?pageIndex=1&pageSize=20&timeRange%5B0%5D=1712641102283&timeRange%5B1%5D=1728365902283) list, click the order details, and check the payment timeout on the details page.![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2854318571/p1006026.png)
