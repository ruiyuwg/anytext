ApsaraMQ for RocketMQ provides public endpoints and virtual private cloud (VPC) endpoints for you to connect your clients to ApsaraMQ for RocketMQ instances. If you use the public endpoint to connect to your ApsaraMQ for RocketMQ instance, you are charged for Internet traffic. This topic describes the billing rules of Internet traffic.

## Supported regions

ApsaraMQ for RocketMQ provides public endpoints in the following regions: China (Hangzhou), China (Shanghai), China (Qingdao), China (Beijing), China (Zhangjiakou), China (Hohhot), China (Shenzhen), China (Chengdu), China (Hong Kong), Singapore, Germany (Frankfurt), UK (London), US (Silicon Valley), and Indonesia (Jakarta).

## Billing rules

**Item**

**Description**

Billable item

Outbound Internet traffic that is generated if you use public endpoints to access ApsaraMQ for RocketMQ brokers.

You are not charged for inbound Internet traffic.

Billing method

Pay-as-you-go

Billing cycle

Fees are calculated on an hourly basis.

Fees are settled every 24 hours.

Billing formula

Internet traffic fees per hour = Total outbound Internet traffic in the hour × Unit price of Internet traffic
