You can delete a DNS record in the Alibaba Cloud DNS console. After you delete a DNS record, the services related to the record will become unavailable. We recommend that you **export the DNS record** before deleting the record.

## Procedure

Log on to the [Alibaba Cloud DNS console](https://www.alibabacloud.com/).

1 . In the left-side navigation pane, click **Authoritative DNS Resolution**.

2.  On the **Authoritative Domain Names**, click a **domain name**.
    

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8140802371/p875192.png)

3 . On the DNS Settings page, click **Delete** in the Actions column of a DNS record. In the message that appears, click **OK**.![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8140802371/p875196.png)
