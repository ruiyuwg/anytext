If you have submitted a contract request, refer to this guide to **view**, **reject**, or **download** a contract.

## **Contract management**

### **View contract**

1.  Log on to the [Expenses and Costs](https://billing-cost-intl.aliyun.com/contract/manage) console.
    
2.  In the left navigation menu, select **Billing >** **Contracts > Contracts**.
    

You can now manage **Framework Agreements** and other agreements. Use the search bar to filter by contract number, creation time, or contract status. ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2501846371/p894026.png)

Click **Contract Details** in the **Actions** column.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2501846371/p894027.png)

**Note**

For **Framework Agreements**, in the Contract Details you can:

-   View your applicable discount details
    
-   Download the complete contract for reference
    

### **Reject a contract**

-   For **Framework Agreements** in Draft or Pending Activation status, click **Reject** to decline the draft contract.
    

**Note**

Once rejected, the contract cannot be reinstated.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2501846371/p894030.png)

### Download a contract

Click the ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2501846371/p873611.png) button to the right of the **Contract Number** to download the contract. ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/2501846371/p894033.png)
