If your account email address is no longer accessible, you should log on to the console and change it immediately. If you cannot log on to change it by yourself, follow the instructions in this topic and submit an appeal to update it.

## Option 1: Change your account email address online

First, try to change your account email address online. For detailed steps, see [Change account logon email](/help/en/account/update-profile).

1.  Log on to Alibaba Cloud with your current account email address and password. Navigate to the **[Security Settings](https://account-console.alibabacloud.com/#/secure)** page in the **Account Center**. Next to your logon account, click **Modify**.
    
2.  Verify your identity. Since your email address is unavailable, you must use your registered **secure phone** for verification.
    
3.  Enter a new, accessible **Logon Email Address** and click **Get Verification Code For Free**. Enter the verification code you receive, and click **OK** to complete the change.
    

If you cannot access your registered secure phone, you may not be able to complete the online process. In this case, follow the steps in Option 2 below to submit an appeal. A support engineer will help you update the email address.

## Option 2: Submit an appeal to update your email address

If both your email address and secure phone are unavailable, the appeal process has two main steps: submitting your appeal information and confirming the appeal result.

### **1\. Submit your appeal information**

1.  Go to the [Alibaba Cloud homepage](https://www.alibabacloud.com/zh?_p_lc=1&spm=a2c45.11132017.9219519220.1.7a51525cqMDPnC). In the upper-right corner, click **Log In**. On the sign-in page, click **Other Sign In Difficulties?**
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/7129607271/p836927.png)
    
2.  Select **Both the registered phone number and email of my account are unavailable** and click **Next**.
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/8703318571/p1006121.png)
    
3.  Complete the slider verification and fill in the appeal form. Click **Submit**.
    
    **Note**
    
    -   Provide your original email address along with a new, accessible phone number and email address.
        
    -   Providing proof of ownership, such as transaction records, will help us process your request and recover your account faster.
        
    
    ![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/7129607271/p837190.png)
    

### **2\. Confirm the appeal result**

After you submit the appeal materials, the page will display a **Query Code** and a link to track its progress. Because changing your email address or phone number is a sensitive operation, our support engineer will contact you to re-verify your identity and payment information. After receiving your authorization, the engineer will update your registered email address or phone number.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/7129607271/p836978.png)

## **What to do next**

You can use the updated email address or phone number to log on to and verify your account. Keep your email address and secure phone number up to date. For more information, see [Change account logon email](/help/en/account/update-profile) and [Change the secure mobile number](/help/en/account/specify-or-change-a-bound-mobile-phone-number).
