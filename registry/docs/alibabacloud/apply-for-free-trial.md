This topic describes how to apply for a free trial of Secure Access Service Edge (SASE).

## Background information

A 7-day free trial is provided. Each Alibaba Cloud account is provided a quota of 100.

## Procedure

1.  [Contact sales personnel](https://www.alibabacloud.com/zh/product/sase?_p_lc=1#J_4569441500) to apply for a free trial.
    
2.  Fill in the form and click **Submit**.
    
    Alibaba Cloud reviews your application within five business days after submission. After the application is approved, you can use the free trial of SASE.
