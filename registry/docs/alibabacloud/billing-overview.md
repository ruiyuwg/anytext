This topic describes Hologres billing methods and how to estimate costs.

## Official Q&A Group Support

Join the Hologres official Q&A DingTalk group for professional and efficient support. For more information, see [How to get online support?](/help/en/hologres/support/obtain-online-support-for-hologres).

## Terms

-   Storage capacity
    
    Storage capacity refers to the data volume stored by users in Hologres (excluding data replicas). Billing is based on actual usage. Storage includes user business data, metadata, and other data. For details, refer to your bill. The unit is GB.
    
-   Computational Workload
    
    Compute capacity for exclusive instances refers to the total compute resources required for operations such as writing and querying in Hologres. The unit is CU (1 CU = 1 core, 4 GB).
    
    Exclusive instances also support the following features:
    
    -   When you execute SQL jobs using the Serverless Computing feature, compute capacity refers to the amount of Serverless Computing compute resources used. The unit is CU×H.
        
    -   When you use the time-sharing elasticity feature to provision additional compute resources, compute capacity refers to the amount of provisioned compute resources. The unit is CU×H.
        
-   Network usage
    
    Network usage refers to the data volume of network transmission when you use the cross-region snapshot replication feature. The unit is GB.
    

## Billing Methods

Hologres exclusive instances offer two billing methods: subscription and pay-as-you-go. Billing includes three resource types: compute, storage, and network, along with related billing items. The following figure shows the details.

![image](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/9886633771/CAEQVBiBgMDukbqR5xkiIDZmNjFjZDQ4YTM1MTQyNTU4M2Y0MzFjNjVhZDg1Y2Zh4431088_20240527114750.656.svg)

**Note**

Exclusive instances under both billing methods support advanced features such as Serverless Computing, backup and restore, and cross-region snapshot replication. Compute, storage, and network resource usage generated by advanced features are all billed on a pay-as-you-go basis.

-   **Subscription**
    
    Estimate the compute and storage resources that your business requires, and access Hologres through a subscription billing model—paying monthly or annually in advance. For more information, see [Subscription](/help/en/hologres/product-overview/subscription-1#concept-2028538).
    
-   **Pay-as-you-go**
    
    Computing fees are calculated based on the purchased computing resource specifications and instance runtime. Storage fees are calculated based on the actual storage usage and storage duration. Fees are billed hourly. This means they are **Charged (and Settled) Once Every Hour**. For more information, see [Pay-as-you-go](/help/en/hologres/product-overview/pay-as-you-go#concept-2389219).
    
    **Note**
    
    Compute resources are billed hourly even if no tasks are running in the instance.

## **Billing calculation**

Hologres uses different billing logic for subscription and pay-as-you-go instances. You can view the pricing details for your region. **For billing rules and when billing stops for each billing item, see** [Billing rules for each billing item](#20f03aedb328v)**.**

Example scenarios:

-   Scenario 1
    
    In subscription mode, you estimate that your business needs 500 GB of Standard storage and 64 compute units (CUs) for 6 months. If your instance is in the Singapore region, your upfront payment is $31.970149 × 64 × 6 + $0.182090 × 500 × 6 = $12,822.81. Infrequent Access storage uses the same billing logic.
    
-   Scenario 2
    
    In subscription mode, you initially bought 100 GB of Standard storage. Due to rapid business growth, your storage usage reached 200 GB in one hour. You are charged extra for the 100 GB overage. If your instance is in the Singapore region, the overage charge is (200 − 100) × $0.000379 = $0.0379.
    
-   Scenario 3
    
    In pay-as-you-go mode, your business uses 64 CUs and stores 100 GB of Infrequent Access data in one hour. If your instance is in the Singapore region, your hourly bill is $0.182090 × 64 × 1 + $0.000055 × 100 × 1 = $11.66.

## Billing cycle

The billing cycles for Hologres are as follows:

-   Subscription: When you purchase a subscription Hologres instance, a bill for the upfront payment is pushed to the instance's Alibaba Cloud account. If your storage usage exceeds the allowance, the excess is billed on a pay-as-you-go basis. A bill for the overage is pushed every hour until your storage usage is back within the allowance.
    
-   Pay-as-you-go: A bill is pushed to the instance's Alibaba Cloud account every hour.
    

## Billing method selection

Hologres offers two billing methods. You can choose one based on the following recommendations and your business needs.

-   Development and testing projects: These projects are suitable for development and debugging by developers and analysts. Jobs are unpredictable, and data volume is small. Use pay-as-you-go billing to control costs and keep resource consumption within limits.
    
-   Stable jobs: After development and debugging, your jobs go live in production. Consider subscription billing.
