This topic describes the billable items, billing methods, and other billing-related information of E-MapReduce (EMR) Serverless StarRocks.

**Description**

**References**

Billable items

EMR Serverless StarRocks has multiple billable items. You can learn the billable items, billing methods, and the formulas that are used to calculate fees from the references.

[Billable items](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/billable-items)

Billing methods

EMR Serverless StarRocks supports the subscription and pay-as-you-go billing methods. You can obtain information about the billing methods from the references.

-   [Subscription](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/subscription)
    
-   [Pay-as-you-go](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/pay-as-you-go)
    

Billing method changing

If the billing method of an existing EMR Serverless StarRocks instance cannot meet your business requirements, you can change the billing method. You can change the billing method only from pay-as-you-go to subscription.

[Change the billing method from pay-as-you-go to subscription](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/pay-as-you-go-package)

Overdue payments

If you have an overdue payment, the system sends you a notification. If you want to continue using your instance, you must settle your overdue payment at the earliest opportunity

[Overdue payments](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/overdue-payments)

Renewal

After a subscription StarRocks instance expires, the running of the instance is affected. If you want to continue using the instance, you can manually renew the instance or enable auto-renewal for the instance before the instance is released.

[Renewal policy](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/renewal-description)

Refunding

If you no longer require a pay-as-you-go instance, you can release or scale in the instance based on your business requirements. If you no longer require a subscription instance, you can request a refund for the instance.

[Refund policy](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/refunds)

Bill viewing

If you have questions about the billing of your StarRocks instances, you can view your bills and billing details in the Expenses and Costs console.

[View bills](/help/en/emr/emr-serverless-starrocks/product-overview/view-bills)
