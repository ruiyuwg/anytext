On September 27, 2024, ApsaraDB for Redis and Tair were merged into a single service called Tair (Redis OSS-compatible). In the future, all services and support will be provided under the Tair brand.

You can continue to use Tair (Enterprise Edition) and Redis Open-Source Edition by means of the console, product page, documentation, and API of Tair (Redis OSS-compatible). Thank you for your attention and support. We will continue to provide you with excellent products and services under the new brand.

For more information about the brand integration, see the FAQ section below. If you have any questions, [submit a ticket](https://smartservice.console.alibabacloud.com/#/ticket/createIndex).

## FAQ

-   Will Redis Open-Source Edition be affected by the change?
    
    Redis Open-Source Edition will not be affected by the change. The change is solely a rebranding and merging of products. Redis Open-Source Edition will become a subcategory under the Tair brand and will continue to provide services. The purchase, renewal, scaling, and usage of Redis Open-Source Edition instances are not affected by the brand integration.
    
-   Is it mandatory to convert Redis Open-Source Edition instances into Tair instances?
    
    No. Redis Open-Source Edition will continue to provide services as a subcategory under the Tair brand. You can still purchase Redis Open-Source Edition instances for your business.
    
-   Will Redis Open-Source Edition continue to be maintained and receive feature updates after the brand integration is complete?
    
    Yes. We will continue to provide services for Redis Open-Source Edition and keep updating its features.
    
-   Will Redis Open-Source Edition versions compatible with Redis 7.4 or later be available?
    
    Yes.
