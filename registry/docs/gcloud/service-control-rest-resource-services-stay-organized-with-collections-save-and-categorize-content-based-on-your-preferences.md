# REST Resource: services Stay organized with collections Save and categorize content based on your preferences.

 

## Resource

There is no persistent data associated with this resource.

## Methods

### `[allocateQuota](/service-infrastructure/docs/service-control/reference/rest/v1/services/allocateQuota)`

Attempts to allocate quota for the specified consumer.

### `[check](/service-infrastructure/docs/service-control/reference/rest/v1/services/check)`

Checks whether an operation on a service should be allowed to proceed based on the configuration of the service and related policies.

### `[report](/service-infrastructure/docs/service-control/reference/rest/v1/services/report)`

Reports operation results to Google Service Control, such as logs and metrics.
